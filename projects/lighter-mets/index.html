Lighter-Mets

Evaluating a Lifestyle Programme to Improve Cardiovascular Metabolic Health

Lighter-Mets is a longitudinal research study designed to evaluate a structured lifestyle intervention for individuals with metabolic syndrome. Cardiovascular and metabolic health are often influenced by interconnected lifestyle habits; this programme aims to address these factors through evidence-based dietary and behavioural support.

Over the course of one year, we aim to investigate how a combination of professional health coaching and digital health tools can support sustainable improvements in metabolic markers. Participants provide vital data through clinical assessments and, depending on their randomized study group, may gain access to personalized coaching and our dedicated research platform.

What will I need to do?

  • Participate in a 1-year longitudinal study.
  • Attend 4 study visits for assessments and follow-ups.
  • May use a specialized research app providing personalized diet and lifestyle insights (depending on group assignment).
  • May engage in one-to-one health coaching sessions (depending on group assignment).

Eligibility

  • Waist Circumference: ≥ 80cm (Female) or ≥ 90cm (Male).
  • Plus at least 2 of the following criteria:
  • Taking medication for diabetes OR fasting glucose blood test ≥ 5.6.
  • Taking medication for high pressure OR systolic BP ≥ 130 / diastolic BP ≥ 85.
  • Taking medication for high cholesterol/triglycerides/LDL OR blood test for triglycerides ≥ 1.7.
  • Taking medication for high cholesterol/triglycerides/LDL OR blood test for HDL cholesterol < 1.29 (Female) or < 1.03 (Male).
  • Not receiving other structured lifestyle modification programs (e.g., seeing a dietician regularly or having a personal trainer).

Benefits

  • $30 reimbursement upon completion of the study.
  • Free blood tests with your results shared directly with you.
  • Free body composition analysis.
  • The opportunity to receive one-to-one health coaching.
  • The chance to use a research app for personalized diet and lifestyle insights.
