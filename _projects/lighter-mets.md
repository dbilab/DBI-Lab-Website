Lighter-Mets is a comprehensive research initiative evaluating the effectiveness of a structured lifestyle programme designed to improve cardiovascular metabolic health in individuals with metabolic syndrome. Over the course of one year, participants will receive detailed metabolic monitoring and, depending on their study group, may also receive personalized support and professional health coaching to achieve sustainable health improvements.
