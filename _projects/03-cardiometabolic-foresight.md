The Cardiometabolic Foresight study is an upcoming research initiative dedicated to the early identification and prevention of cardiometabolic risks. Utilizing advanced longitudinal data and predictive modeling, we aim to identify the subtle physiological signals that precede clinical metabolic conditions.

Our research integrates diverse health markers to establish a holistic, predictive view of individual health, facilitating the development of more effective and personalized preventive strategies in Singapore.
