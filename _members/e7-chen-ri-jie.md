I'm Ri Jie, I completed my Bachelor of Science (Honours) in Nursing at the National University of Singapore (NUS), and I am currently working in the cardiothoracic surgery unit at the National Heart Centre Singapore. My research focuses on digital interventions which aim to enhance sustainability of positive lifestyle behaviour changes to aid in secondary prevention of cardiometabolic diseases. Along with my clinical knowledge, I aspire to be an advocate for my patients embarking on their recovery journey. Outside of work, I enjoy relaxation, driving, and gaming.
