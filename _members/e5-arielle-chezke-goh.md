From nursing school in polytechnic to graduating with a Bachelor of Science (Nursing) with Honours from the National University of Singapore, my journey has inspired me to consider how existing and emerging evidence can make a meaningful difference in people’s lives. My research interest lies in cardiology and women’s health as important gaps remain in how women’s health needs are understood and addressed. With evolving evidence, I value identifying what is working, where knowledge is lacking, and how research can better support early risk assessment, prevention, and clinical care.
